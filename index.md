# Data platforms built for dependable decisions

Block11 is an IT consultancy specialising in the architecture of data platforms and data-processing systems. We help organisations turn complex, growing data estates into reliable foundations for analytics, products, and operations.

## What we do

- Design scalable, secure data-platform architecture.
- Build robust batch and streaming data-processing pipelines.
- Modernise data integrations, storage, and analytics foundations.
- Improve data quality, observability, governance, and operational reliability.
- Support engineering teams with technical strategy and hands-on delivery.

## From blockchain to data platforms

Our early work was rooted in blockchain projects, where resilient distributed systems and trustworthy data flows are essential. Today, we apply that experience to a broader challenge: designing data platforms that remain clear, maintainable, and effective as the business evolves.

Whether you are establishing a data platform, untangling an existing one, or scaling data processing for a new product, we can help.
